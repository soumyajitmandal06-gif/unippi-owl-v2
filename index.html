01
Scientific Scope
What UniPPI-OWL Evaluates — and Explicit Boundaries

        

          

            What UniPPI-OWL Evaluates:
            
    
              
  • Confidence-weighted multi-modal atomic physics: 8-channel Protein–Protein solvation + dedicated 9-channel Protein–RNA/DNA recognition (phosphate salt bridges, base-specific vs. ribose 2'-OH vs. backbone H-bonds, plane-normal π-stacking, 60°-cone cation–π, and Sequence-Specificity Index SSI).
    • 
              
  • Small-molecule pockets, directional halogen bonds (Cl/Br/I, ≥120° σ-hole), inner/outer-sphere metal coordination (Zn²⁺/Mg²⁺/Ca²⁺/Mn²⁺), automatic Ternary Molecular-Glue & Metal-Bridge detection, and Intra-Chain Domain Slicing.
    • 
              
  • 64-point Golden-Spiral Shrake–Rupley SASA, confidence-weighted buried surface area (wBSA), Non-Interacting Surface (NIS) properties, Universal X vs. Y Studio (AF3 / Boltz-1 / Chai-1 exporter), and Welch's t-test Ensemble Diff.
    • 
            

          

          

            UniPPI-OWL Does Not:
            
    
              
  • Replace explicit-solvent molecular dynamics (MD), free-energy perturbation (FEP), or wet-lab ITC / SPR measurements.
    • 
              
  • Use black-box neural voting to hide disagreements between global ipTM and local interface energetics.
    • 
              
  • Transmit or persist any uploaded structure or sequence on external servers (100% local execution).
    • 
            

          

        

      


      

        
02
Frozen Model Architecture
Dual-Layer Physics Engine & Decision Rule

        

          
Locked 5.5 Å Physics Core
Thermodynamic energies (ΔGuni, Kd, UIS) remain strictly invariant to the user's contact-display cutoff by locking physics evaluation to the calibrated 5.5 Å heavy-atom core.

          
IDR-Immune Confidence Damping
Contacts are weighted by wi,j = cA·cB·exp(−max(0, PAE − 3.5)/λ) and cross-chain LIS (PAE ≤ 12 Å), rescuing full-length eukaryotic complexes with long disordered tails.

          
Frozen Threshold
The decision cutoff is locked at UIS = 0.5000 (High-Confidence: 0.6500) with 100% numerical parity verified across 52 CLI ↔ Browser interfaces.

        

      


      

        
03
Orthogonal Interpretation Framework
Independent Biophysical Evidence Tracks

        

          
UniPPI-OWL reports global topology, local PAE coherence, solvation thermodynamics, and structural integrity separately so disagreements remain transparent rather than hidden in a black-box score.

          

            
Track 1 · Global TopologyipTM · Interface pLDDT · % DisorderedWhole-chain structural confidence and flanking disorder fraction.

            
Track 2 · Local CoherenceLIS (PAE ≤ 12 Å) · pDockQPAEEvaluates interface-only PAE coherence immune to disordered tails.

            
Track 3 · Solvation & ContactsΔGuni · wBSA · 9-Ch Atomic MapEnthalpy–entropy compensated solvation plus SB, HB, HP, π, Cat-π, XB & Metals.

            
Track 4 · Integrity & ProvenanceProline Kinks · Clashes · SHA-256Covalent adduct separation, steric audit, and deterministic sequence fingerprint.

          

        

      


      

        
04
Mechanistic Classification
How to Interpret Patterns A–D

        

          

            Pattern A
            
Concordant Rigid / Globular Interactor

            
UIS ≥ 0.5000 + ipTM ≥ 0.60 + ΔGuni ≤ −5.8 kcal/mol. Global topology, local PAE coherence (LIS), and interfacial solvation all support binding.

          

          

            Pattern B
            
IDR-Diluted Local Interactor

            
Strong local LIS (≥ 0.50) & ΔGuni (≤ −5.8 kcal/mol) + Low global ipTM (< 0.60). Rescues true local interfaces diluted by non-interacting disordered tails.

          

          

            Pattern C
            
Thermodynamically Penalized / Weak Packing

            
Chains sit in geometric proximity but are offset by steric clashes, intra-helical Proline kinks, or polar desolvation.

          

          

            Pattern D
            
Below-Threshold Decoy / IDR Hallucination

            
UIS < 0.5000. Low-confidence disordered contacts are damped by >99.9%, locking ΔGuni near the +2.10 kcal/mol cratic barrier.
